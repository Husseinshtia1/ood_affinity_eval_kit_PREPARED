# PREPARED.ai Terms of Service — Draft

Last updated: 2026-05-23

> This document is a draft for product planning and internal review. It is not legal advice and must be reviewed by qualified counsel before public launch.

## 1. Service

PREPARED.ai provides software tools for evaluating BioAI binding-affinity model claims, including out-of-distribution evaluation workflows, reports, audit-oriented outputs, and related hosted or self-managed services.

## 2. No medical, clinical, or regulatory decision-making

PREPARED.ai does not provide medical advice, clinical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, regulatory approval, or laboratory certification. Outputs are decision-support materials for qualified scientific and technical review only.

## 3. User responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

- Ensuring they have rights to upload and evaluate submitted data.
- Avoiding submission of prohibited, unlawful, or unauthorized confidential data.
- Reviewing outputs before relying on them.
- Maintaining account, token, and organization access security.

## 4. Acceptable use

Users may not use the service to:

- Violate applicable laws or third-party rights.
- Attempt unauthorized access or abuse service infrastructure.
- Misrepresent reports as clinical, regulatory, or legal certification.
- Reverse engineer hosted enterprise components where prohibited by license.

## 5. Accounts and organizations

Organization owners and administrators are responsible for managing user access, invitations, roles, and internal governance.

## 6. Reports and outputs

Reports are provided as evaluation artifacts based on submitted inputs and configured evaluation protocols. They may contain errors and must be independently reviewed.

## 7. Availability

The service may be modified, suspended, or discontinued. Production service-level commitments require a separate written agreement.

## 8. Fees and billing

Billing is disabled in the current implementation. Any future paid plans, invoices, renewals, cancellations, refunds, or taxes must be governed by a separate billing policy and provider-backed payment flow.

## 9. Intellectual property

The project follows an open-core/commercial licensing model. Public source availability does not grant rights to hosted SaaS resale, white-label use, or enterprise certification services unless separately licensed.

## 10. Disclaimers

The service is provided as-is unless otherwise agreed in writing. Scientific evaluation results are not guaranteed to be complete, error-free, or fit for a particular regulated purpose.

## 11. Limitation of liability

Liability terms must be finalized by legal counsel before commercial launch.

## 12. Governing law

Governing law and dispute resolution terms must be finalized by legal counsel before commercial launch.
