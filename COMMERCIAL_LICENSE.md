- The scientific evaluation core may remain publicly visible and usable under the repository LICENSE terms.
- Enterprise, hosted, SaaS, certification, billing, managed deployment, and commercial service uses require a separate written commercial agreement.

Without a separate commercial agreement, you may not:

- Sell this software as a product

- Repackage or white-label it for resale

Subject to the repository LICENSE, organizations may use the project for:
